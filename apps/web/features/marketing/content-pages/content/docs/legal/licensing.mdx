## Software license

Current versions of BaseBlocks are source-available under the [Functional Source License, Version 1.1, MIT Future License (FSL-1.1-MIT)](https://github.com/BaseBlocks-HQ/BaseBlocks/blob/main/LICENSE). FSL is a Fair Source license. It is not an OSI-approved open-source license.

Read the license itself for the binding terms. In summary, FSL-1.1-MIT permits use, copying, modification, and redistribution for purposes other than a competing commercial product or service, subject to its patent, redistribution, disclaimer, and trademark clauses.

## Rolling MIT transition

The MIT transition applies separately to every version. A particular version becomes available under the MIT License on the second anniversary of the date that version was first made available under FSL-1.1-MIT. A later release does not inherit an earlier release's transition date; its own two-year period starts when the later release is made available.

For example, if a version is first made available under FSL-1.1-MIT on August 9, 2026, the additional MIT license for that version becomes effective on August 9, 2028. This example does not assign a release date to versions that have not been released.

Versions that BaseBlocks distributed under the MIT License before adopting FSL-1.1-MIT remain available under their original MIT terms. The change does not revoke those permissions.
