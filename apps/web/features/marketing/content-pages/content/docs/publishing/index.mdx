## Publication status

A site is either **published** or **unpublished**. Publishing creates an
immutable version from the saved draft and switches the live surface to that
version atomically. Visibility then determines whether that surface is public or
restricted to signed-in team members. Unpublishing disables the live surface
completely.

## Publishing

<Steps>
<Step>

### Build your site

Create pages and edit content. Changes save automatically.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

Click **Publish** in the editor header. A Public site becomes available to
everyone. A Private site becomes available only to authenticated team members
at its generated BaseBlocks address.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Version history

Open **Version history** to review the changes in a published version. Choose
**Set as live** to roll back visitors to an earlier version without changing the
draft. Choose **Restore as draft** to copy that version into the editor; publish
the restored draft when it is ready.

## Unpublishing

Open the deployment menu and choose **Unpublish**. The generated URL and custom
domain stop serving site content until it is published again. Saved editor
content is not deleted.
