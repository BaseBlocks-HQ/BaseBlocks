## Publication status

A site is either **published** or **unpublished**. Publishing enables its live
surface. Visibility then determines whether that surface is public or restricted
to signed-in team members. Unpublishing disables the live surface completely.

<Callout type="warn" title="Current publishing model">
  Published sites currently read the latest saved page content. Editing a published site can therefore change what visitors see without a separate snapshot deployment. Historical snapshots and rollback are not currently available.
</Callout>

## Publishing

<Steps>
<Step>

### Build your site

Create pages and edit content. Changes save automatically.

</Step>
<Step>

### Publish

Click **Deploy** in the editor header. A Public site becomes available to
everyone. A Private site becomes available only to authenticated team members
at its generated BaseBlocks address.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Unpublishing

Open the deployment menu and choose **Unpublish**. The generated URL and custom
domain stop serving site content until it is published again. Saved editor
content is not deleted.
